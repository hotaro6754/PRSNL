# CyberOS Societal Impact & Education Report
## 1. The Startup Security Gap
Startups cannot afford ,000/yr enterprise EDR solutions like Crowdstrike or Microsoft Defender. Ordinary users are entirely unprotected from Quishing and Smishing on personal devices.

## 2. The CyberOS Intervention
CyberOS shifts the intervention point from **Post-Breach (I4C Reporting)** to **Pre-Credential Submission (Deterministic Block)**.

## 3. Awareness & Education Modules
When a threat is detected, CyberOS does not just block it; it educates.
If Quishing (Malicious QR) is detected, the user immediately receives a micro-lesson mapped to **CERT-In guidelines**:
- *Why is this dangerous?* (QR codes hide the destination URL).
- *What evidence supports this?* (The URL resolved to a known threat IP).
- *What should you do?* (Never scan unverified QR codes in public places).

## 4. Current Workflow Savings
**Manual Workflow:** 6 steps, 4 tools, 15 minutes, high error rate.
**CyberOS Workflow:** 1 step, 1 tool, < 2 seconds, cryptographically verifiable.
